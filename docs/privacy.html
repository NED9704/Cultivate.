Privacy Policy

Cultivate. is an app for keeping a personal library of the books, movies and series you love or want to get to, with private notes on each one. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and in plain language.

Who is responsible

Cultivate. is run by Malak Nasreldin, based in Switzerland ("we", "us"). We are responsible for your personal data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Questions, requests or complaints: support@my-cultivate.com

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

  • Account details: your email address and password. Your password is stored securely in scrambled (hashed) form; we can never see it.
  • Profile: the name you choose to show, if you add one.
  • Your library: the books, movies and series you add, with their title, type, status, author/director/creator, year, cover image link, short description, rating, finish date and the dates you added or changed them.
  • Your notes: we store what you write in the notes area of each item, so the app can show your notes back to you on any device you sign in on. Your notes are private: only you can see them, they are never shown to anyone else (even if you later share your library), and we do not read, analyse or share them.
  • Technical data needed to keep you signed in securely, such as sign-in tokens and basic server logs (for example the time of a request and your IP address).

On your phone, the app also stores your sign-in session and your Light/Dark choice. These stay on your device.

We do not use advertising, tracking, analytics or profiling, and we do not sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your library and notes and show them back to you.
  • To suggest titles, covers and descriptions when you add an item.
  • To keep the app safe and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

Under the GDPR, we rely on performing our agreement with you (providing the app), and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers. They only receive what they need to do their job:

  • Supabase (database and sign-in). Your account, profile, library and notes are stored on Supabase servers in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase also sends our sign-up confirmation emails.
  • Open Library (run by the Internet Archive, USA), Google Books (Google, USA) and TMDB (The Movie Database). When you type a title in the add form, the text you type is sent to one of these services to find matching books, movies or series. Like any website, they also see your device's IP address. Cover images and posters are loaded from their servers. We do not send them your name, email, library or notes.

We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

Data outside Switzerland

Your library is stored in the EU, which Switzerland recognises as providing adequate data protection. Some of our providers are based in, or may process data in, the United States, for example when you search for a title. Where data leaves Switzerland or the EU, we rely on the safeguards the law allows, such as the Swiss–US and EU–US Data Privacy Frameworks or standard contractual clauses used by these providers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. You can delete single items at any time. When you use "Delete my account" in Settings, your account, profile, library and notes are deleted straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong (you can also edit it yourself in the app);
  • delete your data (or use "Delete my account" in Settings);
  • give you your data in a common format so you can take it elsewhere;
  • stop or limit certain uses of your data.

Write to support@my-cultivate.com. We will answer within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you live in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each person read and change their own library. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Age

Cultivate. is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger people. If you think a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date below and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
